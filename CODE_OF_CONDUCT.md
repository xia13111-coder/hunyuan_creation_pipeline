# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participants are expected to communicate respectfully, review ideas on
their technical merits, accept constructive feedback, and make the community
welcoming regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained
personal attacks, and disruption intended to prevent others from participating.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews,
discussions, documentation, demonstrations, and other spaces where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the channel described in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove content, reject
contributions, issue warnings, or restrict participation. Reports will be kept
confidential where reasonably possible, subject to legal and safety duties.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the
need to protect affected participants. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter
is itself a violation of this policy.
